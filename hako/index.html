01 · Why

Most aquascapes are built once and torn out a year later. HAKO is built to change.

Every stone, soil tile and plant pod locks to the same 50 mm grid. A layout takes an hour to build, and nothing is glued, foamed or wedged into place.

When you want something new, lift the pieces out and set them down again somewhere else. The plants stay in their own soil, so they keep growing instead of starting over.

02 · System

Four parts. One grid. Every piece has a place.

  1. 01

    Pod

    A removable pot of soil. It drops into any tile or any stone socket, and lifts out with the roots intact.

  2. 02

    Stone

    Faceted rock with pod sockets and moss pockets. Hollow and flooded, so it sinks on its own.

  3. 03

    Key

    One loose bow-tie joint for everything. It locates rather than locks, so grit and biofilm can't jam it.

  4. 04

    Tile

    A soil tray for carpet plants. Grow one big lawn, then lift out a single square without tearing the rest.

Rescape in a day.

Move the mountain, keep the moss. Three moves, one tool.

01 · Tool

Lift.

Slide the tool down any seam. It cuts the roots that cross it and levers the tile out, carpet and all.

02 · Stone

Move.

Stones lift straight off their keys. Set them anywhere on the grid: a new ridge, a new valley, the same rocks.

03 · Pod

Replant.

Pods drop into any tile or any stone. The plant never leaves its soil, so nothing melts and nothing restarts.
