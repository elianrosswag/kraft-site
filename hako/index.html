02 · System

Every piece has a place.

Four parts and one tool. Everything snaps to the same 50 mm grid, so any pod fits any socket and any stone sits on any tile.

  1. POD

    Plant pod

    A removable pot of soil. Drops into any tile or any stone socket, and lifts out with the roots intact.

  2. STONE

    Stone

    Faceted rock with pod sockets and moss pockets. Hollow and flooded, so it sinks on its own.

  3. KEY

    Key

    One loose bow-tie joint for everything. It locates, it doesn't lock: grit and biofilm can't jam it.

  4. TILE

    Tile

    A soil tray for carpet plants. Grow one big lawn, then lift out a single square without tearing the rest.

03 · Rescape

Rescape in a day.

Most aquascapes are built once and torn out a year later. HAKO is built to change: move the mountain, keep the moss.

01

Lift.

Slide the TOOL down any seam. It cuts the roots that cross it and levers the tile out, carpet and all.

02

Move.

Stones lift straight off their keys. Put them anywhere on the grid: new ridge, new valley, same rocks.

03

Replant.

Pods drop into any tile or any stone. The plant never leaves its soil, so nothing melts and nothing restarts.
